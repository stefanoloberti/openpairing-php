# TRF-2026 individual subset

`Trf2026Codec` reads and writes individual Dutch, Burstein and Round Robin
tournament data using fixed byte columns and exact decimal scores. Round Robin
is available for standings/tie-break calculation; the project does not claim a
Round Robin pairing generator. This is intentionally not a general
team/advanced-record TRF converter.

| Record | Supported fields |
|---|---|
| `012` | Tournament name |
| `022`-`132` | City, federation, dates, checked player counts, tournament/arbiter/time metadata and fixed-column round dates |
| `142` | Number of rounds, 1-256 |
| `152` | Explicit initial colour `W` or `B` |
| `162` | Exact `W`, `D`, `L`, `A`, `P`, `X` point values |
| `182` | Pairing controller identifier |
| `192` | `FIDE_DUTCH_2025`, date-dependent `FIDE_DUTCH`, `FIDE_BURSTEIN`, or `FIDE_ROUNDROBIN` |
| `202`/`212` | Mutually exclusive rank-order declarations consumed by the standings API/CLI |
| `222` | Encoded time control |
| `250` | Exact game virtual points over inclusive round and TPN ranges |
| `001` | TPN, sex, title, name, rating, federation, FIDE ID, birth date, score, rank and round blocks |

Import assigns canonical player ID equal to TPN because record `001` exposes no
separate stable ID. Played result codes are `W/D/L/1/=/0`; the unusual results
draw-loss, loss-draw and loss-loss are accepted, while inconsistent win-draw
results fail closed. Unplayed/bye codes are retained in result history but do
not enter opponent or colour history. Scheduled `w`/`b` colours on `+`/`-`
forfeits are preserved and checked for reciprocity without becoming colour
history. A blank bye result imports as the TRF26-equivalent zero-point bye `Z`
and is written canonically as `Z`.
Every opponent result must have a reciprocal compatible record, played colours
must be opposite, and every reported score must equal the exact sum of its
round results under record `162` (or the official defaults).
Writer output is ASCII and CRLF. Input is limited to 2 MiB, lines to 4096 bytes,
players to 4,096, and unknown record types are rejected rather than dropped.

Record `192` is mandatory in the public TRF-2026 import contract and is never
inferred. Records `142` and `152` follow their TRF26 conditional rules: `142`
is inferred from completed `001` round blocks when possible, and `152` is
inferred from the scheduled first-round colour of the highest-ranked paired
participant. Either record is required when its value cannot be inferred,
including a pre-round-one ITDX file. Corpus verification may normalize a
missing legacy `192` to `FIDE_DUTCH_2025`, but that transformation is reported
by the laboratory harness and is not performed by the product CLI.

Record `250` is individual-only: the game-points field is stored exactly; the
match-points field is not used and is emitted blank. The legacy per-player
record `XXA` is accepted on import and normalized to `250` on output. The
subset does not yet support national ratings,
teams, prohibited pairings (`260`), abnormal points (`299`), bye records
(`240/320`), team records or informative `801/802` records.

The record-by-record individual closure plan and the team/individual boundary
are in `TRF26-INDIVIDUAL-GAP-AUDIT.md`.
